Common questions

Short answers to the things people actually need.

What does TempoTapp actually do?

You create timing by tapping. Warp Audio changes when the music happens so the recording follows that timing while preserving pitch.

The detected clicks are on the off-beat.

Open Advanced Settings in Warp and shift Beat Alignment by ½ beat earlier or later.

The detected pulse is too fast or too slow.

Open Advanced Settings and change Beat Feel to Slower or Faster, then analyze again.

What if the recording is piano, in 6/8, has triplets, or uses rubato?

Use Analyze Audio first. Give TempoTapp the meter, counted pulse, subdivision feel, timing style, pickup information, and—when needed—a few tapped reference beats or the first downbeat. Then review and drag the generated source beat map before warping.

Fit Whole Recording or Match Beginning?

Fit Whole Recording spreads your timing shape across the full recording. Match Beginning maps your clicks to the first source beats, then eases back to the recording’s natural tempo.

Why can extreme warps sound different?

Very large slowdowns or speedups require heavier time-stretching. TempoTapp preserves pitch and cleans tiny clicks, but extreme changes can still sound more processed.

Where are my files saved?

Click tracks and saved warp projects are stored on this device only, inside TempoTapp’s private app storage. Downloaded MIDI click tracks and WAV warp files go to your normal Downloads or Files location.

Privacy: what leaves my device?

Nothing. TempoTapp has no server. Your recordings, sheet music, click tracks, and account details never leave this device. Your library and saved audio are encrypted on this device with a key unlocked by your password, so a signed-out account’s data cannot be read even by someone with full access to the device. For the same reason there is no password reset. The only network activity is loading the PDF and Studio-Quality audio engines, and the app ships with built-in copies so that normally happens offline too.

Where are my files saved?

Your account and library — click tracks, their settings, and saved warped audio — are stored in your TempoTapp account so they appear on every device you sign in on. Downloaded MIDI click tracks and WAV warp files go to your normal Downloads or Files location. Sheet music PDFs and the original recordings you analyze are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

Privacy: what leaves my device?

Your email, name, click tracks, and saved warped audio are stored in your account on Google Firebase servers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules allow only your own signed-in session to read your data; TempoTapp does not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. Recordings you analyze or warp and the sheet music you load stay on your device — only the finished warped audio you choose to save is uploaded. We collect no analytics and set no tracking cookies. Delete account permanently removes everything stored for you.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen press Forgot your password? and we will email you a link to choose a new one. Confirm your email address after signing up (the link is in your inbox) so that reset emails can reach you.

Can I share a click track with students or an ensemble?

Yes. In My Click Tracks, press Share on any track to export a small .tempotap.json file, then send it by AirDrop, Messages, email, or any file-sharing service. Recipients open My Click Tracks → Import shared track on their own device and account. Nothing goes through a server; the file contains only the tap timings and settings, not audio or sheet music.

What does the AI beat tracker do, and where does my audio go?

Analyze can use a small neural network (BeatNet, 1.6 MB) that was trained on thousands of real performances to find beats, downbeats, and the time signature directly from the sound — it handles rubato, piano, and ambiguous meters far better than the classic onset detector. It runs entirely on this device in a background thread; your recording is never uploaded. If the AI engine cannot load, TempoTapp automatically uses the classic detector and tells you. The AI beat tracker uses BeatNet by Mojtaba Heydari, Frank Cwitkowitz and Zhiyao Duan (ISMIR 2021), licensed CC BY 4.0, with a beat-tracking decoder after Krebs, Böck and Widmer (ISMIR 2015).

How do I delete my data?

Open Account → Delete account. That permanently removes your account, every saved click track, and every saved warp project and its audio from this device. There is no copy anywhere else to recover from, so download anything you want to keep first.
